Origin & Academic Equity

Bridging the global academic inequality gap in scientific research.

The vast majority of high-impact research publications are published exclusively in English. For doctoral researchers, professors, and universities in developing countries — particularly across Sub-Saharan Africa and Cameroon — accessing, translating, and assimilating complex academic literature represents an immense hurdle.

Traditional automated translation workflows often struggle to maintain intricate multi-column layouts, displacing mathematical formulas, table boundaries, and figure alignments.

"Access to scientific knowledge should be universal and seamless. Researchers everywhere deserve tools that preserve the exact layout, equations, and typography of peer-reviewed literature without compromising formatting or accessibility. RockTranslate was built by students to empower global education without borders."

— Winter Tony (PerfectWin), Creator of RockTranslate

Engineering & Algorithms

Engineered for Complex Scientific Layouts

A hybrid architecture combining matrix vector decomposition, dynamic typographic adaptation, and a universal AI inference gateway.

Spatial Vectorial DOM Matrix Decomposition

Unlike traditional text extractors that flatten multi-column documents into continuous unformatted streams, RockTranslate harnesses pdf2htmlEX's geometric engine. Every text segment, equation, and figure is mapped with its exact Cartesian matrix coordinates matrix(a, b, c, d, tx, ty).

<div class="t m0 x15 y48 ff1 fs2 fc0 sc0 ws0" style="transform: matrix(1, 0, 0, 1, 142.3, 89.1);">...</div>

100% Offline & Private (Ollama)

Your unpublished thesis manuscripts, pending patent applications, and confidential lab findings never leave your local workstation. Run Llama 4, Mistral, or Qwen directly on your local CPU or GPU.

Dynamic Typographic Kerning & Scaling

When a translated paragraph expands by +25%, RockTranslate dynamically recalibrates horizontal aspect ratios, font sizing, and tracking to ensure no word ever spills over neighboring borders or charts.

Universal AI Model Inference Gateway

Choose your engine based on cost and reasoning requirements: Google Gemini 3.8 Flash / Pro, OpenAI GPT-5.6 Terra, Anthropic Claude 5 Sonnet / Haiku 5, DeepSeek-V4, or any OpenAI-compatible server. Easily switchable with 2 clicks.

from rocktranslate import RockTranslator
 
# Initialize layout-preserved translation engine
translator = RockTranslator(
    model="gemini/gemini-3.8-flash", # or "gpt-5.6-terra", "claude-5-sonnet"
    target_lang="French",
    temperature=0.2
)

success = translator.translate(
    input_pdf_path="elsevier_article.pdf",
    output_pdf_path="elsevier_article_fr.pdf"
)
print(f"Translation complete: {success}")

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers for researchers and laboratories regarding document integrity, data security, and open-source licensing.

Through pdf2htmlEX spatial analysis, each text element is bound to its original geometric bounding box. When translations expand (such as in French or German, where word volume increases by 20–30%), our algorithm computes a proportional micro-adjustment on font size and CSS matrix transform: transform: scaleX(...). Text seamlessly conforms to its allocated spatial slot without colliding with figures or neighboring columns.

During HTML DOM instrumentation, matrix equations, Greek mathematical symbols, sub/superscripts, and vector graphics are isolated and tagged. They are excluded from linguistic translation to ensure they remain mathematically and spatially intact in their original positions.
